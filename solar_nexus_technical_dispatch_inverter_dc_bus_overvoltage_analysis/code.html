Dissecting High-Frequency Transformerless Inverter Topology: Why DC Bus Overvoltage (Error 02) Occurs During Cloud Transients

A rigorous root-cause investigation into perturbation MPPT sampling lag, intermediate DC link capacitor surge headroom saturation, and firmware mitigation loops across Sungrow, Growatt, and Solis hybrid power conversion units.

Marcus Vance, PE verified
Lead PV Power Electronics Architect • IEEE Industrial Applications Society Member
download Download Engineering Brief (PDF)
troubleshoot Diagnostic Executive Summary

Transformerless string inverters employ two-stage topology: a high-efficiency DC-DC boost stage feeding a shared intermediate DC link, inverted into single/three-phase AC by an IGBT or SiC bridge. When rapid cloud-edge optical focusing causes irradiance to jump from 200 W/m² to over 1,350 W/m² in <180ms, the mismatch between MPPT algorithm slew rates and real-time power export dynamics triggers rapid overvoltage saturation in the DC link capacitor bank, tripping Error 02 / OV-DC before safety thresholds are exceeded.

01 // ELECTRODYNAMIC MECHANISMS

The Physics of Cloud-Edge Optical Transients

In field installations across mixed climates, sudden changes in cloud cover are not merely a shift from dim diffuse sky to nominal direct irradiance (1000 W/m²). Instead, the physical phenomenon known as cloud enhancement (forward Mie scattering) acts as an atmospheric prism. Highly localized solar rays reflect off the dense silver linings of cumulus cloud formations, causing instantaneous ground-level global horizontal irradiance (GHI) to exceed 1,350 to 1,450 W/m² for periods ranging from 800 milliseconds to 15 seconds.

Silicon solar cells, governed by quantum photodiode behavior, respond nearly instantaneously (<100 microseconds) to incoming photon flux. The instantaneous current \(I_{pv}\) spikes proportionally:

EQUATION 1: INTERMEDIATE BUS VOLTAGE EQUILIBRIUM
V_{bus}(t) = \sqrt{ \frac{2}{C_{dc}} \int_{0}^{t} \Big[ P_{pv}(\tau) - P_{grid}(\tau) - P_{loss}(\tau) \Big] d\tau + V_{bus}(0)^2 }

Where \(P_{pv}\) is incoming instantaneous DC array power, \(P_{grid}\) is real power injected into the utility line through the bridge, and \(C_{dc}\) is total equivalent capacitance of the electrolyte link.

Under equilibrium conditions, \(P_{pv} \approx P_{grid}\). However, standard Perturb and Observe (P&O) or Incremental Conductance (INC) MPPT firmware operates at discrete cycle intervals—typically sampling every 50 to 200 milliseconds. When the cloud boundary passes:

  • The Array Operating Point Drift: The solar array voltage attempts to settle near open-circuit potential while current surges by 30-40%.
  • Energy Trap at \(C_{dc}\): The DC-DC boost converter chokes the input, but charge stored in boost inductors coupled with current inertia continues to dump coulombs directly onto the capacitor bank.
  • Grid Export Throttling Lag: Anti-islanding algorithms and phase-locked loop (PLL) synchronization constrain how quickly the inverter bridge can ramp AC injection without violating IEEE 1547 / UL 1741 power quality limits.

04 // FIELD REMEDIATION PROTOCOL

Engineering Remediation Step-by-Step

1

Recalculate String Voc Under Sub-Zero Conditions

Verify string sizing using the module's cold-temperature open-circuit coefficient \(\beta_{Voc}\). An oversized string sitting near the 550V ceiling during 10°C ambient temperatures will violate bus safety margins the instant cloud lens refraction strikes.

Checklist: Max PV Voc(T_min) < 0.85 × Inverter Max Bus Rating
2

Measure DC Link Capacitor ESR Degradation with LCR Meter

Electrolytic DC bus capacitors dry out due to thermal stress in enclosed inverters. A 20% degradation in capacitance combined with increased Equivalent Series Resistance (ESR) halves the transient charge buffer, creating false Error 02 trips during moderate irradiance swings.

Acceptable Metric: Dissipation factor D < 0.15 @ 120Hz test frequency
3

Enable "Fast Dynamic MPPT Damping" Firmware Register

Access the advanced installer portal on Solis, Sungrow, or Growatt. Transition the MPPT tracking mode from "High Precision Efficiency" to "Dynamic Irradiance Compensation". This widens the sampling frequency to 20ms and enables feed-forward DC bus voltage clamping before hardware OVP trips.

Firmware setting: Register 0x03B2 [ENABLE_FAST_FEEDFORWARD_CLAMP = 1]

DC Link Capacitor Residual Stored Energy

Even after disconnecting the DC array isolator and tripping the AC breaker, the internal intermediate bus capacitors retain lethal direct-current potentials exceeding 500 VDC. Never open the electronics chassis without observing the mandatory discharge protocol.

Excellent diagnostic breakdown Marcus. We encountered this exact issue on a 48-unit commercial rooftop array in Bavaria. Solis units were repeatedly locking into OV-DC at exactly 13:10 CEST during scattered cumulus transit.
