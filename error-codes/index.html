High-Voltage Electrical Safety Notice

Photovoltaic string arrays generate up to 1000V DC which can be lethal and cannot be shut off while exposed to sunlight. Always de-energize DC isolators, lock out AC disconnects, and use insulated 1000V-rated CAT III/IV tools. Consult a certified solar electrician when in doubt.
